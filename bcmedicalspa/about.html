A clinic, reimagined.

For years we taught aesthetic medicine. Today we practise it — exclusively, and with everything we learned along the way.

Est. as a clinic
Our story

From classroom
to chair.

BC Medical Spa began as a respected training institution, shaping skilled aesthetic practitioners across British Columbia. That history gave us something rare: a deep, first-principles understanding of every treatment we offer.

We've now turned the page. The classrooms are closed and our focus is singular — delivering exceptional, medically-led aesthetic care to our clients. No teaching schedules, no trainees on your skin. Just experienced hands and undivided attention.

What hasn't changed is our standard. The same rigour we once brought to education, we now bring to every consultation, every plan and every result.

What we stand for

Three principles, always.

Precision

Evidence-led treatments performed by trained, experienced practitioners under medical supervision. We measure twice and treat once.

Honesty

We recommend only what genuinely serves you — and we'll happily talk you out of what doesn't. Conservative by default.

Calm

A private, unhurried space designed to feel like a retreat. You'll never be rushed, processed, or treated like a number.

The space

Designed to
disarm.

Warm neutrals, soft light and considered detail — a clinic that feels nothing like one. Step inside and exhale.
