Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you start

I don't know any French. Can I still join?

Yes. Our French Foundations program is designed for complete beginners and helps you build your French progressively from the fundamentals.

How long does it take to reach CLB 5?

The timeline depends on your starting level, learning pace, class intensity and individual progress. After understanding your current level, our team can recommend an appropriate learning pathway.

Do you prepare students for TEF and TCF Canada?

Yes. Our intermediate and advanced programs include focused TEF and TCF Canada preparation, including exam strategies and mock examinations.

Do you offer in-person classes?

Yes. Our focus with in-person classes is to ensure that every student gets the time and attention they deserve to achieve their goals.
