Destination 01

Canada

World-renowned universities, a high quality of life, and some of the most welcoming post-study work pathways anywhere. A natural choice for professional programs including medicine, physiotherapy, dentistry and law.

Why students love it

Diverse, safe cities and strong graduate outcomes.

Popular fields

Health sciences, business, engineering, IT.

Apply for Canada

Destination 02

Australia

Sunlit campuses, world-class research and an unbeatable student lifestyle. Australia pairs globally ranked universities with a relaxed, outdoor culture — and excellent options for professional and vocational study.

Why students love it

Lifestyle, weather and strong industry links.

Popular fields

Healthcare, hospitality, engineering, business.

Apply for Australia

Destination 03

United Kingdom

Centuries of academic heritage and globally respected degrees, often completed in less time. The UK combines historic institutions with vibrant, international cities at the heart of Europe.

Why students love it

Prestige, shorter degrees and rich culture.

Popular fields

Business, law, the arts, sciences.

Apply for the UK

Destination 04

Ireland

A warm, friendly, English-speaking gateway to Europe — and home to the European headquarters of many of the world's biggest technology and pharmaceutical companies. Great for students eyeing a global career.

Why students love it

Friendly culture and strong tech industry.

Popular fields

Technology, pharma, business, data.

Apply for Ireland
