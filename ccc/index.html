Our story

Welcome to Chai Coffee Company

Chai Coffee Company was born out of a generational love for chai and an acquired but now equally intense affinity to coffee! Add to that a Punjabi passion for delicious food and good times.

With Chai Coffee Company, we want to create a space where people can experience the rich aromas and unique tastes of both chai and coffee in a cozy and welcoming environment, indulge in the comforting flavours of these beloved beverages and create their own memorable stories.

Come in for a chat, a catch-up, or simply a chai or coffee!
