Our story

Our Story

Chai Coffee Company was born out of a generational love for chai and an acquired but now equally intense affinity to coffee! Add to that a Punjabi passion for delicious food and good times.

With Chai Coffee Company, we want to create a space where people can experience the rich aromas and unique tastes of both chai and coffee in a cozy and welcoming environment, indulge in the comforting flavours of these beloved beverages and create their own memorable stories.

Over the years, our experiences with beverage culture in India, Australia, and now Canada convinced us that there was a need for specialty tea and coffee in Surrey that has the authenticity of taste and depth of flavour that we know and love.

Come in for a chat, a catch-up, or simply a chai or coffee!

Our specialty

Our Chai

What makes our Chai special is the taste, passion, and dedication that goes into every cup. We have meticulously developed all our Chai recipes considering the versatile tastes and preferences of Chai drinkers. Our homemade Chai Masala is a signature spice mix that has been passed down over three generations.

Our passion

Our Coffee

Here at Chai Coffee Company, we love our chai and coffee equally. All our baristas have been trained to understand those flavours and deliver consistently great-tasting coffee. Our ethically sourced, specialty coffee is freshly ground and comes from two Canadian roasters.
