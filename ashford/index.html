Why Ashford Career College

An accredited path to a thriving career.

We are a designated training institution regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU), offering practical, career-aligned education in cosmetic aesthetics.

Designated & Regulated

Programs are reviewed and approved by PTIRU within the BC Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills.

Career-Ready Skills

Hands-on training on live models with the technology and tools used in modern medical spas and clinics.

Flexible Funding

Interest-free installment plans, approved funding partners, and StudentAid BC eligibility for qualified programs.
