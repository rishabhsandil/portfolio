Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Ashford is a designated private training institution, regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU) within the BC Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills. Designation means our programs meet provincial standards for curriculum, student protection and institutional quality. Read about our designation →
Ashford offers programs across two areas of study: Childcare & Education, and Beauty & Aesthetics. They range from short technician certificates through to the 800-hour Master of Cosmetic Aesthetics diploma, with one course delivered fully online. View all programs →
Request information or submit a registration form, and an advisor will walk you through the requirements for your program. Most programs ask for a Grade 12 diploma or equivalent and English language proficiency sufficient to follow instruction. Some advanced programs have prerequisites, which are listed on each program page. See admission requirements →
New cohorts begin on the first of each month at both campuses. Once your registration is complete you can join the next available intake, so there are no fixed semester start dates to wait for.
Financial assistance may be available to eligible students and programs. Ashford is designated by StudentAid BC, so students in qualifying programs may be able to apply for provincial and federal student assistance, and interest-free installment plans are also offered. Eligibility depends on your program and circumstances, so speak with our team to confirm what applies to you. Explore financial options →
Ashford has two campuses: Coquitlam Centre at 1214-2929 Barnet Highway, and West Vancouver at 1854 Marine Drive. Both are accessible from across Greater Vancouver, and a third campus in Surrey is coming soon. Not every program runs at both campuses, so each program page lists where it is taught, and the Early Childhood Assistant course is delivered fully online. Find our campuses →
