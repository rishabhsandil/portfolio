PTIRU Regulation

Ministry Designation

Ashford Career College is a designated private training institution regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU) within the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills.

PTIRU administers the Private Training Act and its regulations, which set standards and provide consumer protection for students. Our designation confirms that our programs meet these rigorous provincial standards.

Financial Aid

StudentAid BC Eligibility

Ashford Career College is designated by StudentAid BC for eligible programs. Qualified students may be able to apply for Canada and British Columbia student financial assistance — including loans, grants, and other supports.

This eligibility is only granted to institutions that meet specific quality and regulatory requirements, providing further assurance of the quality of our educational offerings.
