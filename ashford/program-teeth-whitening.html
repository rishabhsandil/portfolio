Laser Teeth Whitening Technician

A 5-hour hands-on certification in cosmetic laser teeth whitening — covering laser technology, teeth anatomy, sanitation protocols, and live model practice. No prerequisites required.

Class B — No Prerequisite

Add teeth whitening to your practice.

This hands-on training program covers how teeth whitening lasers work, including the history of this technology. Essential content includes teeth pigmentation, laser technology fundamentals, the biology of teeth, and hands-on practical components including live model procedures. Laser teeth whitening is a popular add-on service that complements any aesthetics or beauty practice.

Skills You Will Develop

What this program covers.

Everything needed to offer professional laser teeth whitening services.

Teeth Types, Shades & Laser Technology Basic Teeth Biology & Anatomy Teeth Pigmentation Reduction Laser History & Operating Principles Sanitation & Disinfection Methods Pre & Post-Laser Treatment Legality of Teeth Whitening Live Model Procedures Client Consultation Equipment Testing & Maintenance
Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Determine teeth types and shades, understand how laser whitening works, and apply laser technology
  • Understand what dentists and non-dentists are permitted to do in relevant jurisdictions
  • Apply sanitation and disinfection methods in a clinical setting
  • Understand basic teeth biology and anatomy
  • Explain teeth rejuvenation concepts and outcomes
  • Perform teeth pigmentation reduction treatments
  • Perform the whitening procedure successfully on live models
  • Describe and understand laser history and operating principles
  • Explain the legality of the teeth whitening business in BC
  • Describe pre and post-laser treatment protocols
  • Perform patient observation on the model
  • Perform consultation and collect medical information from the model
  • Critically analyze equipment testing and maintenance requirements
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract before application is processed
  • International students must obtain a valid education study permit through Citizenship and Immigration Canada

This is a Class B program — no prior aesthetics experience or prerequisite program is required. Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.
