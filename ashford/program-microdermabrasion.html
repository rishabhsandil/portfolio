Technician Program — Class B

Microdermabrasion & Skin Care Technician

A 20-hour certification in high-frequency, galvanic and ultrasonic devices, medical exfoliation, and pore extraction — no prerequisites required.

Class B — No Prerequisite

Master the essentials of skin care.

The Microdermabrasion & Skin Care Technician program teaches students how to operate high-frequency, galvanic, and ultrasonic devices. Students also learn how to effectively use medical exfoliators, pore extraction techniques, toners, and moisturizers. This foundational program builds the hands-on skills needed to deliver professional-quality skin care treatments and serves as the prerequisite for the Advanced Skin Care Program.

Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Perform an aesthetic facial assessment
  • Understand the anatomy of human skin
  • Use toners and moisturizers effectively
  • Perform pore extraction and deep cleansing techniques
  • Conduct initial client consultation and provide after-care guidance
  • Perform medical exfoliating procedures
  • Discuss safety and disposal standards
  • Operate and maintain treatment equipment
  • Use the Diamond tip microdermabrasion effectively
  • Perform microdermabrasion and chemical peel treatments
  • Perform high-frequency treatments
  • Perform galvanic and ultrasonic techniques
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract before application is processed
  • International students must obtain a valid education study permit through Citizenship and Immigration Canada

This is a Class B program — no prior aesthetics experience or prerequisite program is required. Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.

Pathway Note:

This program is the prerequisite for the Advanced Skin Care Program.
