Master of Cosmetic Aesthetics Diploma

An 800-hour comprehensive diploma covering every program we offer — designed for students ready to launch a full-spectrum career in cosmetic aesthetics.

StudentAid BC Funding Available

Become a complete aesthetics professional.

The Master of Cosmetic Aesthetics Diploma allows students to be trained and certified in every course we offer — including numerous skincare, laser, and cosmetic beauty courses. By enrolling in a vast amount of technology and techniques, graduates leave with the skills and tools to be independently successful in the cosmetic aesthetic industry, whether running their own business or securing a position at a cosmetic spa or clinic.

Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Distinguish between facial rejuvenation concepts
  • Discuss and perform skin and laser procedures
  • Distinguish between IPL, Diode, ND-YAG and Teeth Whitening lasers
  • Perform microblading, Ombré, powder & shading brows, and dermaplaning on live models
  • Perform eyelash extension procedures on live models
  • Evaluate and discuss common skin disorders
  • Understand and perform sanitation and disinfectant procedures
  • Evaluate and apply equipment testing and maintenance
  • Distinguish between different facial and body treatments
  • Evaluate and apply client consultation and patient observation
  • Understand and perform after-care procedures
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • All applicants must complete a Student Enrolment Contract before processing

Note: Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.

Start Dates

Year-round enrollment. Classes start on the first of each month. Spots are limited and assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.
