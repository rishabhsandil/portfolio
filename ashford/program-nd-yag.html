Technician Program — Class B

Laser ND-YAG Technician

A 20-hour hands-on certification covering ND-YAG, Picosecond, and Q-Switch lasers for tattoo removal, pigmentation treatment, skin toning, and deep pore cleansing. No prerequisites required.

Class B — No Prerequisite

Specialize in ND-YAG laser technology.

This hands-on training program covers how ND-YAG, Picosecond, and Q-Switch lasers work, including the physics of these laser systems. Students gain an essential overview of skin anatomy and physiology, treatment parameters for photo facials, vascular and pigmented lesions, and tattoo removal — a high-demand specialization in the field.

Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Evaluate and discuss basic skin, hair, biology, and anatomy
  • Distinguish between facial rejuvenation concepts
  • Analyze skin pigmentation reduction techniques and perform treatments
  • Understand and perform sanitation and disinfectant procedures
  • Evaluate and apply ND-YAG laser history and operating principles
  • Describe laser operating safety procedures on clients
  • Distinguish between skin toning, pigmentation removal, and tattoo removal
  • Describe and perform deep pore cleansing with carbon
  • Evaluate and apply equipment testing and maintenance
  • Critically assess laser parameters (wavelength, pulse duration, spot size, energy settings)
  • Distinguish between medical lasers for skin problems
  • Perform skin typing and fluency selection
  • Understand and perform post-laser treatment care for the client
  • Evaluate and apply client consultation and patient observation
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract before application is processed

This is a Class B program — no prior experience or prerequisite program is required. Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.
