Common Questions

Before you enroll

Answers to what students ask most about schedules, entry requirements and paying for a program.

It depends on the credential. Technician certificates run from 5 to 30 hours, typically one week full-time. Advanced certificates run 100 to 120 hours over 5 to 6 weeks full-time. Our Master of Cosmetic Aesthetics diploma is 800 hours over 28 weeks full-time, or 48 weeks part-time. The ECEA 101 course is 32 hours over 8 weeks, online and asynchronous. Every program card above lists its exact hours and schedule.
Yes. New cohorts begin on the first of each month, and you can start as soon as your registration and payment are complete. There is no single intake deadline to wait for.
Requirements vary by program. Entry-level technician certificates are open to beginners. Advanced certificates have prerequisites, which are listed on each program card and detail page. For example, Advanced Laser requires the Laser IPL & Diode Technician certificate or equivalent.
We run two campuses, Coquitlam and West Vancouver. Not every program runs at both, so each card above lists where that program is taught. The ECEA 101 course is delivered fully online.
StudentAid BC is the provincial student financial assistance program. Because Ashford is designated by PTIRU, qualifying students may be eligible for StudentAid BC funding on eligible programs. Our Master of Cosmetic Aesthetics diploma is StudentAid BC eligible.
Yes. We offer interest-free installment plans, for example four monthly payments, and we accept tuition coverage from approved funding providers. Talk to an advisor about what fits your situation.
