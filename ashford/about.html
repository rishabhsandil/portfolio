About Ashford Career College

Ashford Career College is built with a vision to create meaningful career opportunities both within our local communities and beyond. Based in British Columbia, the college is committed to helping students gain practical, industry-focused education that leads to real employment outcomes.

By working closely with employers, instructors, and community partners, Ashford Career College aims to support workforce growth while empowering students to build stable, rewarding futures. As a growing institution, the college is focused on creating an inclusive learning environment where students can gain hands-on skills, confidence, and career-ready training for today's evolving industries.

Ashford Career College believes education should be accessible, supportive, and directly connected to real-world success — helping shape the next generation of skilled professionals.

Designated & Regulated

Education you can trust.

We are a designated private training institution, regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU) within the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills.

PTIRU Designation

PTIRU (formerly PTIB) administers BC's Private Training Act and its regulations, which set standards and provide consumer protection for students. Our designation confirms that our programs meet those standards.

Learn about PTIRU

StudentAid BC Eligible

Ashford Career College is also designated by StudentAid BC for eligible programs. Qualified students may be able to apply for Canada and British Columbia student financial assistance — including loans, grants, and other supports.
