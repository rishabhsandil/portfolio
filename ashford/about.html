About Ashford Career College

A designated private training institution dedicated to empowering the next generation of skilled professionals in British Columbia.

Our Mission

Practical, expert-led career training.

At Ashford Career College, we are dedicated to empowering students through expert-led, practical career training. From laser therapy and skincare to advanced beauty enhancement techniques, our programs are designed to support confident, job-ready skills, conveniently located in the heart of Coquitlam and West Vancouver.

We believe a great aesthetic professional is built through hands-on practice, mentorship from experienced instructors, and a deep understanding of the science behind every treatment. Every program at Ashford Career College reflects that belief.

Designated & Regulated

Education you can trust.

We are a designated private training institution, regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU) within the Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills.

PTIRU Designation

PTIRU (formerly PTIB) administers BC's Private Training Act and its regulations, which set standards and provide consumer protection for students. Our designation confirms that our programs meet those standards.

Learn about PTIRU

StudentAid BC Eligible

Ashford Career College is also designated by StudentAid BC for eligible programs. Qualified students may be able to apply for Canada and British Columbia student financial assistance — including loans, grants, and other supports.
