Early Childhood Education Assistant

Health, Safety, and Nutrition

Build the foundational knowledge to keep children safe, healthy, and thriving. A 32-hour online course for anyone preparing to work with young children in childcare and early learning environments.

Course Overview

What you’ll learn

Study Fully Online: Complete 32 instructional hours asynchronously over 8 weeks, fitting the course around work and family commitments.
Grounded in Real Scenarios: Illness recognition, infection control, indoor and outdoor safety, emergency and allergy response, nutrition and food safety, all at an assistant-level scope of practice.
A Step Toward Certification: Can serve as one of the qualifying courses toward BC ECE Assistant certification, or as a starting point for further early childhood education training.
No Practicum Required: This course is completed entirely online, with no placement to arrange.

What you graduate with

Upon successful completion of the program, students will receive an ECEA Certificate / Certificate of Completion from Ashford Career College.

Requirements for working as an Early Childhood Educator Assistant in British Columbia include certification through the BC ECE Registry. Completion of this course alone does not guarantee eligibility for ECEA certification or employment in a regulated childcare setting. Applicants should confirm current requirements and apply directly with the ECE Registry.
