Designated Institution

Why our designation matters for funding.

Ashford Career College is a designated private training institution regulated by the Private Training Institutions Regulatory Unit (PTIRU). This designation is the prerequisite that makes StudentAid BC funding available for our eligible programs — it confirms that our curriculum, student protection, and institutional quality meet provincial standards.

Designation also reassures approved third-party funding providers that the institution operates under regulated oversight, which can open up additional funding pathways for qualified students.
