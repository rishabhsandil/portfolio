Technician Program — Class B

Cold Body Sculpting & Body Contouring Technician

A 20-hour certification in non-invasive body contouring — covering Cold Body Sculpting, Radio Frequency, Cavitation, and Lipo-Laser with hands-on practical training. No prerequisites required.

Class B — No Prerequisite

Shape and contour with confidence.

This program teaches students to use advanced devices that firm and tighten the skin, reduce stubborn fat cells, and reshape body contours — without invasive procedures. Students learn to apply Radio Frequency technology for both facial and body contouring, Cold Body Sculpting (cryolipolysis), ultrasound cavitation, and lipo-laser. You'll develop the skills to conduct client consultations, select appropriate treatments, and safely deliver results across all body areas.

Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Perform a comprehensive aesthetic body assessment on clients
  • Distinguish between Cold Body Sculpting, Radio Frequency, Cavitation, and Lipo-Laser technologies
  • Describe and evaluate techniques used to reduce fat cells non-invasively
  • Perform client consultation and selection for appropriate body contouring treatments
  • Master safety protocols and discuss possible complications with clients
  • Apply theoretical knowledge to practical treatment measures
  • Perform hands-on practical training with Cold Body Sculpting, RF, Cavitation, and Lipo-Laser devices
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract before application is processed

This is a Class B program — no prior experience or prerequisite program is required. Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.
