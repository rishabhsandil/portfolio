Technician Program — Class B

Dermaplaning Technician

A 10-hour certification in surgical blade dermaplaning — covering facial anatomy, safety, contraindications, and live model procedure practice. No prerequisites required.

Class B — No Prerequisite

Master the art of dermaplaning.

The Dermaplaning course teaches students how to effectively use surgical blades to gently scrape away the top layer of problem skin, removing dead skin cells and vellus hair (peach fuzz) to reveal smoother, brighter skin. Students learn facial anatomy, assessment, treatment indications, safety considerations, and contraindications — then put theory into practice on live models. Dermaplaning is a highly requested treatment that pairs well with other skin care services.

Skills You Will Develop

What this program covers.

Theory, safety, and hands-on practice in professional dermaplaning.

Facial Anatomy Skin Analysis & Assessment History & Treatment Indications Safety Issues & Contraindications Surgical Blade Sizes & Selection Dermaplaning in Skin Rejuvenation Live Model Procedures Individualized Treatment Protocols
Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Describe and discuss facial anatomy in the context of dermaplaning
  • Identify components of history, assessment, skin analysis, and treatment indications on models
  • Critically evaluate safety issues, contraindications, complications, and precautions of dermaplaning
  • Distinguish between surgical blade sizes and select appropriately
  • Discuss dermaplaning as a skin rejuvenation treatment with clients
  • Demonstrate the dermaplaning procedure on live models
  • Develop individualized care plans and treatment protocols for clients
Admission Requirements

How to qualify.

  • Minimum age: completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID required (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract before application is processed
  • International students must obtain a valid education study permit through Citizenship and Immigration Canada

This is a Class B program — no prior aesthetics experience or prerequisite program is required. Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.
