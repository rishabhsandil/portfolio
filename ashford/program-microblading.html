Microblading & Ombré Brows Technician

A 30-hour certification mastering microblading and Ombré/powder brow techniques using manual tools — with live model practice and a complete starter kit included. No prerequisites required.

Class B — Starter Kit Included

Create flawless brows with precision technique.

Our Microblading & Ombré Brows program teaches students to master these high-demand techniques using manual tools, creating the finest strokes and small dots that leave a flawless result. Students learn to create any desired brow shape or size, how to insert pigment into the skin with control, and how to control tool depth. The program covers both microblading (hair-stroke technique) and Ombré/powder brows (machine shading), two of the most sought-after services in cosmetic beauty today.

Learning Objectives

What you will be able to do.

  • Evaluate and describe the anatomy and physiology of the skin
  • Evaluate hair follicles and pigments on clients
  • Distinguish between semi and permanent makeup techniques
  • Understand colour theory and select appropriate pigments
  • Perform brow mapping and draw a perfect brow outline
  • Distinguish between proper and improper strokes
  • Distinguish between different needle and blade types
  • Understand touch-up protocols and schedule recommendations
  • Communicate after-care procedures to clients
  • Conduct client consultation and waiver overview
  • Discuss pricing strategies and profit considerations
  • Perform the microblading and Ombré procedure on live models
