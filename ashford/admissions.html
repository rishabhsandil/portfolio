Eligibility

Admission Requirements

  • Completed grade 12 or be a minimum of 19 years of age
  • Photo ID (Driver's License, Passport, or BCID) to prove identity
  • English Language Proficiency proof if applicable (IELTS 5.5 or equivalent)
  • Completed Student Enrolment Contract
  • Valid study permit for international applicants (Citizenship and Immigration Canada)

Ashford Career College nor the student applicant may waive the admission requirements.

Prerequisites for Advanced Programs

  • Advanced Laser Program: Requires completion of Laser IPL & Diode Technician (20h) or equivalent from a recognized institution
  • Advanced Skin Care Program: Requires completion of Microdermabrasion & Skin Care Technician (20h) or equivalent
  • Advanced Laser & Skin Care Program: Requires completion of Aesthetic Dermatology Technician (30h) or equivalent
  • Master Diploma & Class B Technician Programs: No prerequisite — open to all eligible applicants

Applicants presenting credentials from another institution must provide proof the program was at least the required duration and covered the relevant fundamentals.

FAQ

Common Questions

When can I start?

New cohorts begin on the first of each month. Spots are limited and assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.

How long are programs?

Technician certifications can be completed in days to weeks; the Master Diploma is 28 weeks full-time or 48 weeks part-time.

Do I need experience?

No prior aesthetics experience is required. Our programs are designed for both newcomers and professionals expanding their skills.

What credential do I receive?

Successful graduates receive a diploma or certification from Ashford Career College, recognized as a Designated Private Training Institution.
