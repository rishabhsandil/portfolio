About Us

Restoring authenticity to Indian food from the ground up is the mission that drives us. Spice it Indian is the realization of a dream to serve Authentic Indian food in Canada.

At Your Service

We aim to celebrate the very best of Indian cuisine and to provide a memorable dining experience made possible through our talented chefs and dedicated staff.

After several years in the restaurant business, we decided now was the right time to take all that experience and synthesize it into a local eatery where people can enjoy exquisite food, support local businesses, and eat the kind of food they can feel good about.

We are open for Dine In and we can’t wait to see you!
