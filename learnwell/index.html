Frequently asked questions

Useful information to get you started

Do I need any French to book the consultation?

No. Our A1 Foundation program is designed for complete beginners. We assess your level before you start and place you in the right program, and the consultation is where that happens.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Every new student gets a free placement consultation, with no credit card and no obligation to enrol. If Learnwell isn't the right fit, we'll tell you.

Is this program recognized in Canada?

Yes. Learnwell's exam prep programs prepare students for the TCF and TEF, the official French language tests recognized by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Will this help me get a job or permanent residency?

Our levels map to the NCLC benchmarks used by Express Entry and Francophone Mobility. In your consultation we'll show you which score your pathway rewards and plan the route to it.

How long do the programs take?

Each level varies. If you want to move faster, the Accelerated French Program takes you from A1 to B2 in nine months in a small, focused group setting.

Online or in person?

Both. Cohorts run hybrid, with evening and weekend schedules designed for working adults and newcomers balancing a job, a family and a move.
