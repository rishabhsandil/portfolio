Our Story

BOLD, AUTHENTIC, MEDITERRANEAN FOOD

Welcome to Albasha Express — the vibrant heart of Mediterranean street food right here in Kitsilano, Vancouver! We serve up boldly flavored dishes that redefine fast-casual dining, bringing the authentic tastes of the Middle East to your table.

Whether it's our made-to-order falafels with their crispy, seasoned exterior and fluffy inside, our harmoniously flavored shawarma freshly sliced and served with hummus and tahini, or our freshly made hummus and tzatziki — we use only the finest ingredients to produce these mouth-watering creations.
