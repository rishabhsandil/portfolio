Commercial Pilot License

Canada

A 52-week program combining 80 hours of commercial ground school with 155 hours of flight time across dual instruction, solo flying and time building, delivered through a Transport Canada approved Flight Training Unit. You'll need to be at least 18 and hold a valid Private Pilot License to begin.

  • Commercial ground schoolAir law, meteorology, navigation, systems and flight operations.
  • Dual and solo flight training65 hours of instruction and solo flying in advanced maneuvers.
  • 90 hours of time buildingCross-country flying that builds real command time in your logbook.
  • Safety and regulatory complianceSafety management systems and the standards operators expect on day one.

Career paths: Commercial Airline Pilot, Cargo Pilot, Corporate Pilot, Charter and Bush Pilot, Flight Instructor, Aerial Survey Pilot.
