Pharmacy Assistant Certificate

Canada

A 33-week (620-hour) certificate covering medical terminology, pharmacology, pharmacy math and dispensing software, finishing with a practicum placement in a working pharmacy. Study in class, online or a mix of both, with CPR, First Aid and WHMIS completed along the way.

  • Pharmacology fundamentalsDrug classifications, uses and the medical terminology you'll use daily.
  • Pharmacy math and dispensingAccurate calculations, prescription intake and inventory handling.
  • Pharmacy softwareHands-on training in the dispensing systems Canadian pharmacies run on.
  • Practicum placementReal experience in a community or clinical pharmacy before you graduate.

Career paths: Community or retail Pharmacy Assistant, Clinical Pharmacy Assistant, Hospital Pharmacy Assistant, Pharmacy Clerk, Health Care Consultant.
