3 respected universities in the Caribbean, matched to your grades, budget and goals.

Saba University School of Medicine (SUSOM)

Saba, Dutch Caribbean

Founded in 1992 with 3,500+ graduates. Basic science is completed on Saba before clinical rotations in the U.S. and Canada, with a 98% first-time USMLE Step 1 pass rate (2024).

  • Doctor of Medicine (MD) Program4 years (10 semesters): basic science on Saba (semesters 1-5), then clinical rotations in North America (semesters 6-10).
  • Pre-Medical Master's ProgramFull-time (8 months) or part-time, on-campus or online; completion makes you eligible for the MD program.
  • Gateway ProgramFull-time over 15 weeks, on-campus or online.

Medical University of the Americas (MUA)

Nevis, St. Kitts and Nevis

Founded in 1998 with 1,800+ graduates and a 5:1 student-to-faculty ratio; 93% three-year U.S. residency placement rate.

  • Doctor of Medicine (MD) Program4 years (10 semesters): basic science on Nevis, then clinical rotations in the U.S. and Canada.
  • 5-Year BSc/MD ProgramTwo degrees in five years: three pre-med semesters (on-campus or online), then the MD program.
  • 6-Year BSc/MD ProgramTwo degrees in six years: six pre-med semesters (on-campus or online), then the MD program.
  • Pre-Medical Master's ProgramFull-time (8 months) or part-time, on-campus or online.
  • Gateway ProgramFull-time over 15 weeks, on-campus or online.

St. Matthew's University School of Medicine (SMUSOM)

Grand Cayman

Established in 1997 with 2,300+ graduates; 100% three-year residency placement rate and 97% first-time USMLE Step 2 CK pass rate (2024).

  • Doctor of Medicine (MD) Program4 years (10 semesters): basic science on Grand Cayman (semesters 1-5), then clinical rotations in North America (semesters 6-10).
  • Pre-Medical Master's ProgramFull-time (8 months) or part-time, on-campus or online.
  • Gateway ProgramFull-time over 15 weeks, on-campus or online.
