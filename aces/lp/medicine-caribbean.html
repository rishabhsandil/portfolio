Where you could study

Medicine in the Caribbean.

3 respected universities in the Caribbean, matched to your grades, budget and goals.

Saba University School of Medicine (SUSOM)

Saba, Dutch Caribbean

Founded in 1992 with 3,500+ graduates. Basic science is completed on Saba before clinical rotations in the U.S. and Canada, with a 98% first-time USMLE Step 1 pass rate (2024).

  • 4-Year Doctor of Medicine (MD) ProgramTen semesters: basic science on Saba (semesters 1-5), then clinical rotations across North America (semesters 6-10).

Medical University of the Americas (MUA)

Nevis, St. Kitts and Nevis

Founded in 1998 with 1,800+ graduates and a 5:1 student-to-faculty ratio; 93% three-year U.S. residency placement rate.

  • 4-Year MD ProgramTen-semester Doctor of Medicine program: basic science on Nevis, clinical rotations in the U.S. and Canada.
  • 5-Year & 6-Year BSc/MD ProgramAccelerated pre-med-to-MD pathways for students who want a foundational science year (or two) before the MD program.
  • MBBS to MD PathwayAdvanced-standing transfer for MBBS graduates seeking MD-equivalent standing to practice in the U.S. and Canada.

St. Matthew's University School of Medicine (SMUSOM)

Grand Cayman

Established in 1997 with 2,300+ graduates; 100% three-year residency placement rate and 97% first-time USMLE Step 2 CK pass rate (2024).

  • MBBS to MD PathwayDirect-entry advanced standing for MBBS graduates: basic science on Grand Cayman, clinical rotations across the U.S. and Canada.
