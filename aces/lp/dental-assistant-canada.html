Dental Assistant Diploma

Canada

A 36-week (1,120-hour) diploma covering applied dental sciences, radiography, preventive procedures and dental office management, with 160 practicum hours of direct patient care. Morning and evening schedules available, and graduates are eligible to sit the National Dental Assisting Examining Board (NDAEB) exam.

  • Applied dental sciencesAnatomy, dental terminology and the science behind chairside practice.
  • Dental radiographyTake, process and interpret dental images safely and accurately.
  • Chairside and preventive careAssisting, infection control and preventive procedures with real patients.
  • 160 practicum hoursDirect patient care placement inside a working dental office.

Career paths: Certified Dental Assistant, chairside assistant in general or specialty practice, hospital dental assistant, dental sales, dental laboratory or dental office roles. Around $33/hour on average.
