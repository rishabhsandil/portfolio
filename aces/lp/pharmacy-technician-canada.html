Pharmacy Technician Diploma

Canada

A 55-week (1,380-hour) diploma covering pharmacology, pharmaceutical calculations, sterile compounding and both community and hospital dispensing, with 360 hours of work experience split between community (200 hours) and institutional (160 hours) pharmacy. Accredited programs meet the national competency requirements you need to register with the college of pharmacists in your province.

  • Pharmacology and calculationsAnatomy, physiology, drug therapy and precise pharmaceutical math.
  • Sterile compoundingAseptic technique and compounding practised in a real lab setting.
  • Community and hospital dispensingOperations, dispensing and patient care across both settings.
  • 360 hours of work experience200 hours in community pharmacy and 160 in institutional pharmacy.

Career paths: Pharmacy Technician in hospital, community and compounding pharmacies, plus roles with insurers, government drug plans, regulatory bodies and educational institutions. Up to roughly $36/hour in BC and $41/hour in Alberta.
