Health Care Assistant Certificate

Canada

A 26-week (745-hour) certificate that develops the personal-care, communication and clinical skills to become a frontline care provider, with supervised placements in hospitals and long-term care facilities.

  • Personal care & clinical skillsComprehensive training in personal care, communication and safe, person-centred practice.
  • Supervised clinical placements270 practice hours in hospitals and long-term care facilities to build real confidence.
  • Registry recognitionGraduate eligible for the BC Care Aide & Community Health Worker Registry.
  • Flexible schedulingWeekday, evening or weekend options, full-time or part-time.

Career paths: Health Care Assistant, Community Health Worker, Residential Care Aide, Home Support Worker. Roughly $28/hour with a 96% graduate employment rate.
