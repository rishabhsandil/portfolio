About Us



Indian Aroma offers an exquisite platter of authentic Indian cuisines. Our extensive menu features mouth watering recipes, made with passion and served with love. Indian Aroma embodies in itself the true spirit of food from different regions of India, encapsulating the richness of their colours and flavours.

We aim to see customers enjoy authentic Indian food just as if they were dining in India. We prepare a mix of healthy, delicious and affordable food, topped with a friendly customer service. Visit us at 612 Fisgard Street, Victoria, BC and relish the unique aromas of India.
